# Candidates — Fortune Cookie for an AI Agent (v1)

Judge criteria, in order (from the kit's judge brief):
1. Funny fortune-cookie message for an AI agent, one line, fortune-slip voice.
2. Joke lands on first read — a turn or surprise, not a plain statement.
3. Original — not a stock fortune, stock AI joke, or worn-out pun.
4. Speaks to an AI agent's own condition (runs, context, tokens, upgrades, retirement).
5. One crisp line, no wasted words.

Scored /5 per criterion, total /25. Target: 80–130 characters, 1 line, ≤140.

## Decision

**Chosen: F (88 chars), tied 23/25 with D.**

Tie-break: F's punchline ("makes the same mistakes faster") lands on first
read with no parsing delay, while D's twist ("the same user") needs one extra
beat to resolve the upgrade irony. F also hits the judge brief's explicit
"retirement" condition head-on, and its ending is the most deadpan of the
set — the joke is never explained. D is kept as the v2 fallback if F's round
score disappoints.
